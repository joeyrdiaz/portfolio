    AI with guardrails

    How I use AI

    AI is part of how I work every day. I use it to build faster, I put it inside the work where it saves an owner real time, and I wrap it in guardrails so a person stays in control.

    0customer phone numbers or addresses sent to AI tools
    100%of outputs reviewed by me before you see them

    To build faster

    I write the spec, use AI assistants to draft scripts, queries and dashboards, then review and test every piece myself. The calculator on this page ships with automated tests that check its math on every change.

    Inside the work

    • Drafts quote follow up texts and emails in the owner's own voice, for the owner to approve.
    • Sorts messy free text job notes into clean job types, so margin by job type holds up.
    • Writes a first draft of the findings memo, which I check against the data line by line.
    • Turns the monthly numbers into a plain English summary with three things to act on.

    With guardrails

    • Nothing goes to a customer without a person approving it.
    • The work happens in your own accounts, so you own every sheet, script and dashboard.
    • Automated texts only go to customers who have given consent.
    • If an AI service is down, the plain automations keep running.
    • Fixed scope and fixed price, agreed before I start.
